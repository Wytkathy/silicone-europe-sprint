# Evidence-Led Outreach Playbook

Do not send these training drafts. Re-check every source and replace all
fictional Silora Works claims before real use.

## Personalization Formula

1. One verified observation from the account's official site.
2. One clearly labelled relevance hypothesis.
3. One concise idea or useful question.
4. One low-friction next step.

## Role-Based Value Propositions

- **Category buyer:** Build a coherent reusable-storage range with clear options
  for format, color, packaging, and commercial positioning.
- **Product developer:** Move from concept to reviewed samples using explicit
  requirements, revision records, and test-plan checkpoints.
- **Brand lead:** Translate a visual identity into a coordinated family of
  forms, colors, packaging, and product stories.
- **Quality lead:** Agree evidence requirements before claims are made or
  production decisions are locked.

## Follow-Up Sequence

| Timing | Purpose | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Relevant introduction | One source-backed observation and one question |
| Day 5 | Add value | Share a one-page concept or useful category comparison |
| Day 12 | Close the loop | Ask whether to pause, redirect, or speak later |

## Objection Practice

- **No reply:** Add genuinely useful information once; do not repeatedly chase.
- **Existing supplier:** Ask about unmet gaps, backup needs, or future review
  timing without criticizing the incumbent.
- **MOQ mismatch:** Be transparent; explore range consolidation only if viable.
- **Sample request:** Confirm requirements, claims, testing, ownership, cost,
  timing, and evaluation criteria before promising.
