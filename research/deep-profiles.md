# Ten Priority Account Profiles

These are research exercises based on public category signals. Every commercial
opening below is explicitly a **hypothesis**, not evidence that the company is
seeking suppliers.

| Account | Public signal | Opportunity hypothesis | First question to validate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Koziol | Design-led reusable household assortment | A distinctive color-led storage range could fit its design language | Who owns external product-development partnerships? | https://www.koziol.de/ |
| Manufactum | Durable, considered household assortment | A documented longevity and repair/replace story may matter more than low price | What evidence is required for new food-contact products? | https://www.manufactum.com/ |
| Avocadostore | Sustainability-positioned marketplace | Relevant sellers may reveal unmet reusable-storage niches | Which brands/categories show strongest engagement? | https://www.avocadostore.de/ |
| WENKO | Home and kitchen organization range | Stackable silicone formats may complement organization products | Is private-label or external development used in kitchen ranges? | https://www.wenko.com/ |
| Westwing | Design-led home assortment | Exclusive seasonal colors may create a differentiated capsule | How are exclusive home-accessory collections sourced? | https://www.westwing.de/ |
| OTTO | Large home and kitchen assortment | Marketplace/category data could reveal formats and price bands | Is direct supply or marketplace entry the realistic path? | https://www.otto.de/ |
| Tchibo | Rotating collections | A compact seasonal set may fit a themed kitchen campaign | What lead times and commercial proof are required? | https://www.tchibo.de/ |
| Zwilling | Food storage and kitchen tools | Silicone accessories might complement existing storage systems | What product gap exists that does not dilute the brand? | https://www.zwilling.com/de/ |
| GEFU | Functional kitchen tools | A utility-first reusable storage design could fit | Which recurring kitchen problem is not served by its range? | https://www.gefu.com/ |
| KitchenCraft | Broad kitchen accessories | Multiple formats and packaging variants may suit range development | Which subcategory has a renewal or sustainability priority? | https://www.kitchencraft.co.uk/ |
